## Scope

Home page (`src/app/page.tsx`), the whole single-page portfolio. Mode: Persuade (recruiters decide and act: resume, email, LinkedIn, repos).

## Audience and job

Recruiters skim on a laptop or phone and leave with the resume or a contact link. Engineers check depth through projects and live demos. Proof is the real projects, roles, and links only.

## Constraints

Brief-pinned: blues plus hints of black, cold air and deep water, clean modern first, ASCII and grain as sparse accents, aria-hidden decorative ASCII, reduced motion respected, Figma drafts before code. Code-led build (no image generation); Figma frames act as the design reference.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a cold white print carrying deep blue exposed bands that sink deeper as you scroll, cold air into deep water. It refuses the two-column sidebar portfolio and the near-black neon developer page.

OWN-WORLD: Paper #F2F6FB with ink #0A1A3F; signal #2B4BFF for every action; frost #A8C4FF and ice #E8EFFF on deep #0A1A4A; abyss #040A1C close. Hubot Sans wide uppercase display, Mona Sans body, Martian Mono labels. Ordered-dither band edges, a fine grain, halftone fills on hover, hairlines, no shadows beyond the header pill.

STORY: In one viewport the visitor learns who Aryan is, what he builds, and where the resume is. Scrolling, they read a friendly About, scan dated roles, sink into four projects with live links, skim skills, and land on a dark close with the email in large type.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed band, sky from signal at top to deep at the bottom. Mono kicker top left under the header pill. Name in Hubot Sans 900 at about 9vw, one line on desktop, two on phones, ice white. Title and tagline below at left, then a white "View resume" button and email, LinkedIn, GitHub links. Lower 45% is the ASCII sea with one horizon line; the pointer leaves a fading wake. Bottom edge dithers into paper.

FORM: Cyanotype prints, candidate 5 of 7 on the ordered list; seed key 98a7a090. Raises: horizon (retro grid), density encodes attention (moire gallery), spot-ink discipline (risograph), character cells (split-flap), fading wake (plankton).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable moment

Moving the mouse across the hero leaves a wake in the ASCII sea; scrolling, the bands develop from dither into deeper and deeper blue.
